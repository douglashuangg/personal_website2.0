## MTE546 Multi-data Sensor Fusion

Why do we even need to fuse sensor data? If you've ever tried using a sensor to measure something multiple times, you'll notice the measurements may vary. Consequently, how do we get confident enough in our measurements to make decisions, like for autonomous driving in vehicles. Fusing sensors together helps to reduce noise and uncertainty, as you can never be 100% certain. This course goes over the math and algorithms to do this.

There are 3 main topics in this course:
1. Extracting information
2. Fusing information
3. Autonomous decision making

## Fusing Information
* Combining information from multiple sensors to get a more accurate estimate of the environment

![alt text](image-1.png)

### Weighted Sum Fusion
* You weight each sensor reading based on which sensor you trust more

### Bayesian Filtering
* Follows predict and update cycle. The predict step uses motion model to guess where robot is now based on where it was. Then the update step takes a new sensor measurement to correct the guess.
We get both an estimate and how confident we are in that estimate.
